Methodology

CountyIQ inherits Atlas discipline.

The analytical layer should never weaken the source, geography or comparability controls of the underlying national data layer.

1. One statistic at a time

Rankings are computed only within a single indicator, common period and common definition. GCP size, GCP growth and budget absorption remain distinct.

2. Periods stay visible

2024 GCP, FY2024/25 expenditure and June 2022 voters can appear together as context, but are never blended without an explicit modelling step.

3. Direct vs derived

Published values carry an A badge. A simple transformation such as 2023→2024 GCP growth carries a B badge because it is derived from two published observations.

4. No unsupported attribution

County outcomes are not automatically treated as governor performance. Leadership scorecards require their own methodology, source set and causal limitations.

5. Composite scores are optional

If a CCPI is eventually reintroduced, it should be a transparent derived product with published weights, normalization, missing-data treatment, sensitivity analysis and version history.

6. Geography is inherited

CountyIQ uses Atlas geography IDs and can later drill to constituency or ward only for indicators explicitly eligible at those levels.
