# Kenya Data Atlas — User-facing results

The public product should surface the analytical outputs created by the completed CountyIQ phases, while implementation policy and validation machinery remain in technical documentation.

## Released outputs

### County history and breadth
- 12-year county fiscal history and common-period comparisons.
- Health and living-standard observations with uncertainty retained.
- Education, economy, agriculture and infrastructure/connectivity indicators.

### County rankings and trends
- Complete national leaderboards for every indicator that passes the ranking gate.
- National percentile and population-size peer position.
- Short- and medium-term trend output where comparable history exists.

### Strengths and development gaps
- County strengths relative to national/peer benchmarks.
- Areas needing attention.
- Reproducible "what changed" statements from published observations.

### Development snapshot
- Current 0–100 constrained development snapshot.
- Published five-band relative position for all 47 counties.
- Exact composite rank shown only as a diagnostic position, with plausible weighting rank range.
- No longitudinal composite ranking is published.

### Fiscal delivery and accountability
- FY2024/25 county fiscal-delivery score and ranking across execution, revenue mobilisation and arrears control.
- 46 complete scores; Narok remains explicitly unscored because a required pending-bills input is not submitted in the source table.
- Wage-ceiling and audit signals remain visible context rather than scored inputs.

### Administration-period scorecards and recognition
The Rankings route includes a full 47-county scorecard table showing current fiscal rank/score plus baseline-to-latest changes in overall and development absorption.
- FY2021/22 baseline to FY2024/25 change in overall and development absorption.
- Six reproducible recognition categories: current fiscal delivery, improved overall absorption, improved development absorption, OSR target attainment, lowest pending-bills burden and wage-ceiling compliance.
- Presentation is county-administration-period based; it is not a personal governor causal score.

### County evidence
- 47/47 county evidence profiles.
- Third-generation CIDP coverage and official planning, budget, Controller of Budget, CFSP/CBROP and audit doorways where indexed.

## Public information architecture

The top-level **Rankings** route is the consolidated release surface for these outputs. It should present results first. Technical policy registries, executable rules, validators, schema details and build-system terminology belong in repository documentation and machine-readable data products, not in the primary user interface.
