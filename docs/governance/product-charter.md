# Product charter

## Mission

Make Kenya's public statistics searchable, geographically coherent, comparable, downloadable and traceable from headline to original evidence.

## Primary user promise

> Search for Kenya, a county, constituency, ward or indicator and immediately understand what reliable data exists, what it means and where it came from.

## Scope

The Atlas will provide:

- a canonical Kenya → County → Constituency → Ward registry;
- geographic profiles and maps;
- documented indicators and historical series;
- transparent comparisons, benchmarks and rankings;
- source releases, citations, downloads and APIs;
- explicit quality, estimation, revision and missing-data status.

The Atlas will not:

- claim to be an official government publication without written authority;
- imply affiliation with or endorsement by a source agency, the United Nations, UNDP or a future buyer;
- manufacture small-area precision;
- copy county values into constituency or ward profiles;
- publish identifiable household or personal records;
- replace historical values destructively;
- introduce an undocumented composite county score;
- offer causal judgments unsupported by the underlying evidence;
- allow AI-generated claims to bypass the structured data and review system.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Source, do not obscure.** Every value resolves to a source and release.
2. **Missing is better than misleading.** Absence is labelled plainly.
3. **Geographic method is visible.** Direct, derived, spatial and modelled values never appear equivalent.
4. **History is preserved.** Revisions create new vintages.
5. **Facts are separated from interpretation.** Context is descriptive and attributable.
6. **Comparable means compatible.** Rankings and comparisons require aligned unit, period, level and method.
7. **Public utility includes accessibility.** WCAG 2.2 AA is the target.
8. **Official Kenyan sources take priority** when an equivalent official series exists and is reusable.

## Initial audience

- members of the public;
- journalists and civil-society organizations;
- researchers and students;
- county and national public servants;
- businesses and investors;
- developers using downloads or APIs.

## Success measures

Success is measured across four dimensions:

- **Utility:** successful searches, profiles viewed, comparisons, downloads and citations.
- **Quality:** current observations, review pass rate, correction latency and unresolved issues.
- **Coverage:** validated indicators and direct-data coverage by geographic level.
- **Trust:** complete citations, transparent revisions, user-reported issues and documented resolution.

Usage growth never overrides correctness, privacy or source restrictions.

## Definition of a publishable observation

A publishable observation has an approved indicator, series, value or explicit missing status, unit, reference period, versioned geography, observation method, provenance class, source release, publication date where known, ingestion date, quality status and recorded approval.

## Current ownership and independence

The private-citizen creator is the current Owner/Publisher and owns this charter. The Atlas is an independent public-interest project, not an official government or United Nations publication. Material changes require a decision record, impact assessment and version update.

If the project is later acquired or formally sponsored, charter ownership transfers only through the documented institutional-transition process. A prospective relationship must never be described as completed before a binding agreement and authorized public announcement exist.
