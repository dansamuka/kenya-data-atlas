# Kenya Data Atlas — Data Rights & Reuse Notice

The repository's MIT License applies to **software code**. It does **not** grant or imply a blanket license over third-party source data reproduced, transformed, indexed or referenced by Kenya Data Atlas.

## Source data

Underlying statistical and administrative data originate from multiple public bodies and external publishers. Copyright, database rights, terms of use, attribution requirements and redistribution permissions may differ by source.

Before redistributing a dataset or substantial extract:

1. identify the relevant dataset and source in `data/catalogue/registry/`;
2. follow the recorded source URL and originating agency;
3. review the source publisher's current reuse terms;
4. preserve source attribution, reference period, methodology and any limitations carried by the Atlas;
5. do not imply that Kenya Data Atlas or its software license changes the source publisher's rights.

## Atlas-authored transformations and metadata

The Atlas publishes reproducible transformations, identifiers, provenance relationships, quality badges, comparison rules and analytical outputs. Reuse of those elements should preserve a citation to Kenya Data Atlas and, where they incorporate third-party source data, the underlying source attribution as well.

## No official endorsement

Kenya Data Atlas is an independent public-interest project. Inclusion of a public agency's data or link does not imply endorsement, partnership or official publication status for the Atlas itself.

## Citation

Machine-readable citation metadata are provided in `CITATION.cff`. For reproducible research, cite or archive the exact Git commit/release used in addition to the original data sources.
